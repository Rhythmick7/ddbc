# Never give up

With three matches to go the Huddersfield Winter League Denby Dale team faced the prospect of another season in division 3....I said so in my team blog reasoning that 2 out of our 3 last matches involved games against the top two teams.
Little did I know that we would win our last three games 8 nil and secure the league title. 
So a lesson for me, when you think it's all over its not!
