# Denby Dale Bowling Club Finals Day

The bowls club finals day took place on what was the wettest day of the summer. 

The membership were out to see some top class bowling in difficult conditions from the heavy rain and enjoy the refreshments provided. 

The pairs competition was a long close game, Mike Pearson and Joy Stainthorpe running out winners over Bill Shaw and Adrienne Harmon. 

In the ladies singles Nicky Widdecombe was a worthy winner over Helen Samson 21-14. 

The men’s final was very close with Paul Hutchinson eventually getting the edge over Andy Wilson 21-20. 

Congratulations to all of the winners.
