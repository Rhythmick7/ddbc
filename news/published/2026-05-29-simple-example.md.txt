# Simple way to submit news items for the club

On 29th May 2026 Mick Jennings introduced a very simple mechanism for members to submit news items of interest to other members.

Members can fill in a very simple form which will be sent to the secretary. 

If they want to include photos they can download a simple file then send it by email (along with the photos) to the secretary.

Full instructions at the top of the news page.
