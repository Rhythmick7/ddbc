# Denby Dale Bowler Selected to Represent Barnsley Vets

We are informed that Neale Wallace has been selected to represent Barnsley as part of a Vets team in the Hickson Trophy on 3rd July.

Each team comprises 12 bowlers from each Yorkshire District. To be selected to play at this level is a worthy honour.

The first round is a round Robin between 4 teams, the winning team progressing to the final the following week.  

We'll keep the club posted on progress or otherwise. 

More details can be found on [HuddWeb](https://www.huddweb.co.uk/post/hickson-trophy-2026)
