# Our green at Denby Dale

I play in three teams at Denby Dale so I am able to visit many different bowling clubs and greens in Huddersfield and Barnsley.

Many of them are hidden away - Google satellite view is a useful resource in locating them - and there is a lot of variation in the quality of the green, the type of clubhouse and the attractiveness of the surroundings. Many are connected to political clubs, miners welfare clubs or are part of sports venues, particularly cricket clubs. Others are council-owned and are tucked away in the corner of a park. Some have a bar in the clubhouse and can host social functions.

The demographics tend to be towards the older end of the age spectrum but some bowling clubs have a strong youth section. Most clubs encourage social bowling through round robins and internal competitions and this helps to ensure that new members are welcomed.

I think that we are very fortunate with our club: the setting of the club adjacent to the cricket club and with the infant River Dearne flowing alongside is very attractive. The clubhouse is small but satisfies our needs very well and the green itself is maintained in superb condition - a fact that those of us who have played on inferior greens are very grateful for. 

Alongside the green our most valuable resource is our membership - friendly and welcoming, ready to help out with green maintenance, take part in social bowling, internal competitions and play in teams.

The club website is an integral part of the club and plays an important role in keeping members informed of what is going on in the club. Members are invited to make their own contributions to the news section.
