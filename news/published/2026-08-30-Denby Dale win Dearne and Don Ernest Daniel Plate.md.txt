# Denby Dale win Dearne and Don Ernest Daniel Plate

On a day with forecast rain and thunderstorms, and with one of our team seriously injured in hospital, Denby Dale headed to Farnley Tyas for the finals day.

Penistone A proved their worth in the semi-final but Denby Dale squeezed through by just 1 point to make the final against Meltham A where the team won more comfortably.

Thanks to all those who turned out to support the team, as well as the reserves who proved critical throughout the season.
