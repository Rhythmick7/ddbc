# How to create a news item for publishing

This file is a template.
Blank lines create a new paragraph.

Copy it, rename it, and replace the content with your news story.

This is how you add simple formatting;

ITALIC like this : *ITALIC*
BOLD words are marked like this : **BOLD**
BOLD AND ITALIC : ***BOLD AND ITALIC***

Insert images as follows - no spaces in filenames please !

![Image description](//media/image-name.jpeg)

To insert an email address...

## Dates

The dates at the top (between the lines) control when the news item appears on the website.

- **start:** first day it should show  
- **end:** last day it should show  (set it to the far future if you want it to stay around forever)
