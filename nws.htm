Denby Dale Bowling Club News Items

Denby Dale Qualify for Huddersfield Winter League Finals Day

Neale Wallace and Mick Jennings have qualified for the finals day of the Huddersfield Winter League Pairs KO on Friday 21st February. The finals day will be at Thorpe Green Bowling Club and the quarter final starts at 11:00. If anyone wants to go along to support please contact Neale or Mick for details.

Stuart Simmons won through the first round of the Huddersfield Winter League Singles KO. The round of last 16 will be played on March 30th and we hope Stuart will get through to the finals day on April 6th.

Good line, length and luck to all.
